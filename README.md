# 🚀 CareerForge – Smart Career Development & Assessment Platform

CareerForge is a modern web-based career development platform designed for students and job seekers to improve their skills, prepare for assessments, build professional resumes, and explore career opportunities in one centralized system.

The platform combines:
- Career Management
- Assessment Preparation
- Resume Generation
- Job Exploration
- Community Interaction
- Performance Tracking

into a single integrated ecosystem.

---
